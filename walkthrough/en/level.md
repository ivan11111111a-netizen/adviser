# Your level

Your level decides what Adviser talks about and what it leaves out.

| Level | Talks about | Leaves out |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner | logic, edge cases, error handling, names | architecture, performance |
| Intermediate | plus function structure, duplication, types | large-scale architecture |
| Advanced | everything | — |

You can change the level at any time: the `adviser.level` setting.
