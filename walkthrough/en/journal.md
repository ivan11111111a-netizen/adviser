# Mistake journal

Adviser remembers every mistake: its type, whether you got to the solution on your own, whether you fixed it. If a mistake comes back for the third time in a week, it reminds you and shows the previous case.

In the Journal side panel (the cap icon on the left):

- **week**: how many issues and how many of them you fixed on your own, without opening the solution;
- **frequent mistakes** in three weeks, with a link to each case;
- **mastered**: mistakes that have not come back for three weeks.

The journal is stored only on this computer and is never sent anywhere. The model gets only the five most frequent mistake types, so that it explains them especially clearly.
